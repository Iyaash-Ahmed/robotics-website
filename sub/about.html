About Robotics

Robotics is the intersection of science, engineering and technology that produces machines — called robots — that replicate or substitute for human actions.

Why it matters

Machines that are shaping our future

From factory floors to deep space, robots take on tasks that are dangerous, repetitive or simply beyond human limits — working faster, safer and around the clock.

As artificial intelligence advances, robots are becoming more capable and more collaborative, working alongside people in hospitals, homes, farms and beyond.
