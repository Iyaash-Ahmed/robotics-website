What is entertainment robot?

robot that is not made for utilitarian use, as in production or domestic services, but for the sole subjective pleasure of the human

security robot

Humanoid robots are either remotely controlled or pre-programmed to perform mid-air flips and rolls like actual humans, but with mechanical precision. Movie-makers can use these robots to shoot intense action scenes using CGI and modern image-capturing technologies to create high-quality visuals for audiences to enjoy.

Entertainment Robots are those which are purposely designed to serve the purpose of entertaining humans rather than for any practical use. These robots consist of sensors, microphones for voice identification, and cameras for face recognition so that they can interact with humans.

The entertainment robot category continues to grow due to the lower costs of robot technologies. Today, robot toys can range from $50 to as high as $1,000, but over the next 10 years we expect the average price for toys to come down significantly, making robot toys affordable for more households
