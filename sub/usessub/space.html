What is a Space robot?

Historically, the role of robotics in space exploration has been significant due to the uninhabitable conditions of non-terrestrial planets in the solar system.

Space Robots

There are many uses robots in space. Spacecraft that explore other worlds, like the moon or Mars, are robots and are included in the “Planetary Robotics”. These include orbiters, landers and rovers to explore other planets and asteroids.

Remotely operated vehicles (ROV) and remote manipulator system (RMS) are the two major types of space robots. A typical ROV can be a rover moving over the terrain upon landing, a lander operated from a stationary point and is in contact with an extra-terrestrial plain or an unmanned spacecraft.
