Advantages of Robots

Avoids Accidents

Humans are often another form of error. And we’ve already seen that robots are good at avoiding them. Accidents happen with people at the helm. In the worst case, people will be injured.

More Efficiency

Industrial robots can do certain tasks faster and more efficiently than humans because they are designed and built to perform them more precisely.

Longer Working Span

Humans require breaks in the working day, distractions occur, and attention spans are slow, while robots work 24 hours a day, 7 days a week and can continue to work 100%.

How Does NASA Use Robots?

NASA uses robots in many different ways. Robotic arms on spacecraft can move large objects in space. Robotic spacecraft can visit other worlds. Robotic airplanes can fly without a pilot aboard. NASA is studying new types of robots. These will work with people and help them.
